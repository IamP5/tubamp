# Modulated-delay reading notes: ValhallaSpaceModulator and Flair

Scope: static reading only (disasm.py). Dumps and helper scripts are in this directory
(`d_*.txt`, `pb.txt`, `ctor.txt`, `ens.txt`, `tzf.txt`, `dbl.txt`, `sym.txt`, `rd.py`, `ana.py`).

Conventions. `V` = ValhallaSpaceModulator binary, `F` = Flair binary. `this` = JUCE processor
object; the DSP object `VPlug_SpaceModulator` lives at `this+0x220` ("base"). Offsets below are
from `this` unless written `base+`. Constants that are immediates in code (mov/movk) have no pool
address; their instruction address is given. Pool constants give a `--floats` command.

## 1. ValhallaSpaceModulator

### 1.1 Code layout
- Only `ProcessBlockUp` (mode 0) is a named function [decompiled: V VPlug_SpaceModulator::ProcessBlockUp 0x3c1d0]. The other 10 modes are inlined into `processBlock` [decompiled: V ValhallaSpaceModulator::processBlock 0x19310, size 18404].
- processBlock reads the mode from `this+0x228` and jumps through a halfword table at 0x17078a [decompiled: V processBlock 0x193f8; table bytes via `python3 rd.py "$V" 0x17078a 11 h`]. Index > 10 falls to mode 0. Handler addresses: Up 0x19404 (calls ProcessBlockUp), Up/Down 0x1941c, TZF+ 0x19af0, TZF- 0x1a27c, Tri 0x1aa08, Ocho 0x1b0c8, Doubler 0x1bde8, VariUp 0x1c378, VariUpDown 0x1c7e4, Ensemble360 0x1cc4c, Symphonic 0x1d350 [decompiled: V processBlock 0x19404..0x1daa8].
- Mode names come from the editor's help-text strings, matched to the editor jump table at 0x1707c2 [decompiled: V ValhallaSpaceModulatorEditor::setModModeString 0x26fd4]. Order (index: name): 0 Up, 1 Up/Down, 2 TZF+, 3 TZF-, 4 Tri, 5 Ocho, 6 Doubler, 7 VariUp, 8 VariUpDown, 9 Ensemble360, 10 Symphonic [inferred from the editor jump table; the processBlock and editor tables agree on index 0 = Up and index 1 = Up/Down].
- Parameter-to-mode map: CalcParameter takes `floor(p)-1`, clamps at 0, accepts 1..10 as itself and everything else as 0 [decompiled: V VPlug_SpaceModulator::CalcParameter 0x3b874..0x3ba24]. The mode parameter range is 1..12, default 1 [decompiled: V ValhallaSpaceModulator ctor, pool 0x185c80; `--floats 0x185c80 4`]. So values 1..11 select modes 0..10 and 12 wraps to mode 0 [inferred].
- No oversampling and no latency reporting seen in SetSampleRate or processBlock [decompiled: V VPlug_SpaceModulator::SetSampleRate 0x3bc3c]. SetSampleRate only clears the delay lines and recomputes coefficients; not determined whether processBlock sets a latency elsewhere (no such call seen in the 18 KB function).

### 1.2 VMod_DelayLine
- Buffer is a `malloc(0x20000)` = 131072 bytes = 32768 floats, mask 0x7fff [decompiled: V VMod_DelayLine::VMod_DelayLine 0x3af90..0x3afb8]. At 48 kHz that is 0.683 s [inferred]. Ten lines exist (cleared in SetSampleRate at 0x3bc7c..0x3bd10, stride 0x1448 bytes) [decompiled: V SetSampleRate].
- Per line the write pointer is decremented each sample and the buffer is accessed `buf[(wr + k) & mask]` for k samples old. Write happens before read, so minimum delay is 1 sample (the code adds +1 to every delay) [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c528..0x3c584 (write), 0x3c914..0x3c960 (decrement), +1 sample added at 0x3c478].
- Fractional read is a first-order allpass interpolator, not linear and not cubic. For delay D: `n = trunc(D)`, `f = D - n`, `x = f - 0.5`, `a = P(x)`, `y = buf[n+1] + a*(buf[n] - y_prev)`, and `y_prev = y` (state kept per line) [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c5c0..0x3c644]. Two samples are read per output.
- `P(x)` is a 4th-order Taylor polynomial of the Thiran coefficient `a = (1-f)/(1+f)`: `P = ((((64/243)x - 0.395062)x + 0.592593)x - 0.888889)x + 0.333333`. Constants: 64.0 and 243.0 as immediates at 0x3c5d0/0x3c5dc, -0.395062 (0xbeca4588) at 0x3c5e8, 0.592593 (0x3f17b426) at 0x3c234, -0.888889 (0xbf638e39) at 0x3c23c, 0.333333 (0x3eaaaaab) at 0x3c244 [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp]. Max error against the exact coefficient is 0.0082 at x = ±0.5, 0.0003 for |x| <= 0.3 [inferred, computed by me from the constants]. Delay at low frequency is therefore n + f; at high frequency the allpass phase delay departs from n + f (the usual allpass interpolation behaviour) [inferred].
- The same polynomial and allpass read appear in all 11 modes (constants 64.0 / 243.0 / -0.8888889 / 0.5925926 / 0.3333333 found in each handler by my scan `python3 ana.py`) [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1941c..0x1d350].

### 1.3 LFO
- Phase accumulator: 32-bit unsigned. Top 8 bits index a 256-entry table, low 24 bits are the fraction (`frac = low24 * 2^-24`, 0x33800000 at 0x3c290). Output is a linear interpolation between entry i and i+1 [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c284..0x3c2e4]. Increment per sample = `int(rate_hz / sr * 2^32)` computed as `fcvtzs(rate*256/sr, #24)` [decompiled: V CalcParameter 0x3b6f8..0x3b71c]. A negative rate gives a negative increment, so the sweep runs backwards (reverses barberpole direction) [inferred].
- Tables are built in the constructor (257 entries each, one wrap entry): ramp `i/256` at this+0xdd8 (entry 256 = 1.0) [decompiled: V ValhallaSpaceModulator::ValhallaSpaceModulator 0x2139c..0x213e4]; Hann window `0.5*(1-cos(2*pi*i/256))` at this+0x11dc, entry 256 = 0 [decompiled: V ctor 0x214bc..0x2154c, cosf calls 0x214dc..0x21518]; sine `sin(2*pi*i/256)` at this+0x5d0 [decompiled: V ctor 0x2127c..0x212fc]; triangle (4 quarter segments of 64 entries) at this+0x9d4 [decompiled: V ctor 0x2133c..0x21688]. Symphonic has its own private copies (sine at this+0x19f8, triangle at this+0x1dfc, ramp at about this+0x2200) for its two extra LFOs [decompiled: V ctor 0x2158c..0x216d4].
- Up mode: four phases at 0, 64, 128, 192 of 256 (0x40 steps at 0x3c2e8, 0x3c320, 0x3c354) share one accumulator [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp]. Delay of voice k = `|manual + depth * (1 - ramp_k)| + 1` samples (smoothed depth and manual); ramp is a sawtooth 0..1 [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c390..0x3c3dc]. Voice crossfade gain = Hann(phase_k) [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c2c4..0x3c37c; 0x3c514..0x3c5b0]. Since Hann(p) + Hann(p+0.5) = 1, the two voices of one channel crossfade with unity sum [inferred].
- Left output = w0*y(line0, phase 0) + w2*y(line1, phase 128). Right = w1*y(line2, phase 64) + w3*y(line3, phase 192). So left/right sawtooth phases are 90 degrees apart [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c6a8, 0x3c800..0x3c808].
- Pitch shift implied for Up: ratio = 1 + depth_ms/1000 * rate_hz (delay falls by `depth` samples per cycle) [inferred]. At the parameter maxima (50 ms, 10 Hz) that is 1.5 [inferred].
- Ensemble360: one sine accumulator (rate), phases 0 and +64; line delays `|depth*L_k + manual*tap_k| + 1` with L = u(p), u(p+0.25), 1-u(p), 1-u(p+0.25) where `u = 0.5*(sin+1)` (unipolar sine at 0/90/180/270 degrees) [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1cc94..0x1cd90].
- Symphonic: same 4-line topology as Ensemble360, but delays are driven by two further sine LFOs: A at `0.618*rate + 0.07` Hz and B at `0.5*rate` Hz, B also at +90 degrees; per-line LFO = A, B, B+90, 1-A [decompiled: V CalcParameter 0x3b72c..0x3b788 (rates; constants 0.618 = 0x3f1e353f at 0x3b724, 0.07 = 0x3d8f5c29 at 0x3b730), processBlock 0x1d3c4..0x1d4e0 (use)].
- TZF+, TZF-, Doubler: random smooth LFO. At each phase wrap a new uniform random target in [-1,1) is drawn (`rand() * 2^-31 * 2 - 1`, Doubler at 0x1be3c..0x1be5c); between targets the value follows `prev + (next-prev)*(3p^2 - 2p^3)` implemented as `0.5 - 0.75u + 0.25u^3`, `u = 1-2p`, constants -0.75 at 0x1c304 and -3.0 at 0x1c310 [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1be3c..0x1bea4, 0x1c304..0x1c310; same pattern TZF+ 0x19b24..0x19b64]. Segment rates from CalcParameter: `rate/sr`, `(0.618*rate+0.07)/sr`, `2*rate/sr`, `1.618*rate/sr` [decompiled: V CalcParameter 0x3b798..0x3b90c; 1.618 = 0x3fcf1aa0 at 0x3b7b4].
- Tri: 2 lines and the triangle table [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1aa08.. (only buffers 0x4298 and 0x56e0 and table 0x9d4 referenced; my scan `python3 ana2.py`)]. Details of the antiphase relation not read.
- Ocho: 8 phases at 32-step spacing (0x20..0xe0) using the ramp table, 8 line pairs worth of code [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1b0c8..0x1bde8; my scan]. Windowing/gains not read.
- VariUp, VariUpDown: use `frexpf`/`ldexpf` (stubs 0x16f208/0x16f280) on the delay, i.e. some power-of-two quantisation [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1c4e8..0x1c530]. The "glitch shifting" mechanism is not determined.
- Up/Down: same as Up but the right channel uses the opposite sweep direction (phase offsets 0x80 present) [inferred from the help text and the offsets; not fully traced].
- Ensemble tap multipliers (delay of line k = depth*lfo_k + manual*tap_k): positive manual uses {10, 8.5179, 7.2556, 6.1668}; the sets for other modes (Symphonic {6.89?,...}, TZF {0.98, 0.49, 1.0, 0.5}, Doubler {0.618, 1.0}) are loaded from literal pools `--floats 0x185ba0 24` and `--floats 0x185c90 16`. The CalcParameter store map from the pools to this+0x49c..0x4d0 was read, but which exact set each mode gets is partly [inferred] [decompiled: V CalcParameter 0x3b8cc..0x3b968; pools 0x185ba0, 0x185bc0, 0x185bd0, 0x185be0, 0x185c90]. The help text says "manual delay is 10X displayed value" for Symphonic and Ensemble360, consistent with the 10.0 first tap [decompiled: V string at 0x197347].

### 1.4 Knob mapping
All six parameters share `VMod_Parameter` (0x58 bytes) holding min, max, a curve exponent and the normalised value [decompiled: V setParameter 0x1f900].
- Mix: 0..100 %, default 50, linear [decompiled: V pool 0x185c30; `--floats 0x185c30 4`].
- Rate: -10..+10 Hz, default 0.2 Hz. Bipolar power curve: for normalised v > 0.5, `rate = 10 * (2(v-0.5))^(1/0.3)`; mirrored below 0.5. Exponent 1/0.3 = 3.33 [decompiled: V setParameter 0x1f9f0..0x1fa1c; pool 0x185c40 (-10, 10, 0.2); 0.3 = 0x3e99999a at 0x229c8..0x229d0 of the ctor].
- Depth: 0..50 ms, default 10 ms, linear (curve exponent 1.0 stored at ctor 0x21220). `depth_samples = depth_ms * sr / 1000` [decompiled: V pool 0x185c50; CalcParameter 0x3b7c0..0x3b7d8].
- Feedback: -100..+100 %, default 0. `fb = pct * 0.01 * 0.99 + 1e-4`, so about ±0.9901 [decompiled: V CalcParameter 0x3b7e0..0x3b81c (0.99 = 0x3f7d70a4, 1e-4 = 0x38d1b717); pool 0x185c60]. No smoothing of fb or mix: they are written directly in CalcParameter.
- Manual: -50..+50 ms, default 0, linear; `manual_samples = ms * sr / 1000`. Negative manual reaches through-zero via the `fabs` in the delay formula [decompiled: V pool 0x185c70; SetSampleRate 0x3bd3c..0x3bd48; delay formula ProcessBlockUp 0x3c39c].
- Mode: 1..12, default 1 (see 1.1).
- Wet/dry: equal power, `theta = mix% * 0.01 * pi/2`, `wet = sin(theta)`, `dry = cos(theta)` via `__sincosf_stret`; output = `dry*in + wet*processed` [decompiled: V CalcParameter 0x3b69c..0x3b6d4, ProcessBlockUp 0x3c8cc..0x3c90c]. Dry uses the raw input, not the pre-emphasised one [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c8d4].

### 1.5 Smoothing
- Depth and manual are smoothed per sample with a one-pole `s += c*(target - s)`, `c = 1 - exp(-1000/(300*sr))`, i.e. time constant 300 ms [decompiled: V SetSampleRate 0x3c0ac..0x3c0c4 (300.0 = 0x43960000 at 0x3c0ac, -1000.0 = 0xc47a0000 at 0x3c020 in the same function); use in ProcessBlockUp 0x3c24c..0x3c27c]. A second coefficient with 100 ms time constant is stored at base+0x308 [decompiled: V SetSampleRate 0x3be14..0x3be34]; its consumer not traced.
- The damping coefficient base+0x318 follows depth: `c = 1 - exp(-2*pi*fc/sr)` with `fc = 50000/(min(|depth_ms|,25) + 0.1)` Hz (500 kHz at depth 0, 4.95 kHz at 10 ms, 1.99 kHz at 25 ms) [decompiled: V SetSampleRate 0x3bdc4..0x3be10 (25.0 fminnm, 0.1 = 0x3dcccccd, 50000 = 0x47435000, -2*pi = 0xc0c90fdb)]. Used in the Ensemble/Symphonic feedback low-pass (see 1.6) [inferred from the smoothed value feeding the one-pole at ens.txt 0x1d1ec..0x1d24c].
- No LFO-rate smoothing seen: the increment is replaced on parameter change [decompiled: V CalcParameter 0x3b6f4..0x3b71c].

### 1.6 Loop, filters, saturation
- Up mode, per line: delay output -> 10 Hz first-order high-pass -> `0.5*tanh(2v)` -> times `fb` -> added to that line's own input (no cross-feedback) [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c6b8..0x3c718 (HP), tanhf call stub 0x16f5f8 at 0x3c6fc, store of `0.5*tanh(2v)` at 0x3c704]. High-pass: `H = (1 - z^-1)/(1 + a z^-1)`, `a = (1-K)/(1+K)`, `K = 2*sr*0.0159155` (10 Hz) [decompiled: V SetSampleRate 0x3be38..0x3be6c; constant 0.0159155 = 0x3c82613d at 0x3be44; coefficient pair (1.0, -1.0) in pool 0x185670, `--floats 0x185670 2`]. Stub identification: `otool -Iv` lists 0x16f5f8 = _tanhf, 0x16f4e4 = _rand, 0x16f5ec = _tanf, 0x16f058 = ___sincosf_stret.
- Ensemble360 and Symphonic: four lines, each line output -> 10 Hz HP -> `0.5*tanh(2v)` -> mean subtraction: `v_k - 0.5*sum(v)` (a 4x4 Householder matrix) -> one-pole low-pass (the damping coefficient above) -> times `fb` -> back into the lines [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1d0f0..0x1d24c]. Outputs: left = `0.7071*(y0+y1)`, right = `0.7071*(y2+y3)` [decompiled: V processBlock 0x1d250..0x1d260; 0.7071 = 0x3f350481 at 0x1cc5c].
- Input pre-emphasis and output de-emphasis (all modes seen): input per channel goes through `x + g1*(x - AP1(x))`, where AP1 is a first-order allpass with `a = (tan(u) - 1)/(tan(u) + 1)`, `u = 6685.31/sr` (corner about 2128 Hz), `g1 = 1.4905` [decompiled: V SetSampleRate 0x3c038..0x3c06c (6685.31 = 0x45d0ea7a at 0x3c048; tanf call at 0x3c058; g1 = 0x3fbec9e2 at 0x3c03c); ProcessBlockUp 0x3c3e4..0x3c42c]. High-frequency gain of this shelf = 1 + 2*g1 = 3.98 (+12 dB) [inferred]. After the lines the wet sum goes through the same structure with `tan` scaled by 0.2512 (corner about 534 Hz) and `g2 = -0.3744`, high-frequency gain 1 + 2*g2 = 0.2512 (-12 dB) [decompiled: V SetSampleRate 0x3c078..0x3c0a8 (0.2512 = 0x3e809bcc at 0x3c088; g2 = 0xbebfb21a at 0x3c078); ProcessBlockUp 0x3c870..0x3c8c8]. The two shelves have different corners, so the net wet path is not flat between about 0.5 and 2 kHz [inferred; not measured].
- Noise: each line input gets `(rand()*2^-31 - 0.5) * 3.162e-5` added (about ±1.6e-5, -96 dBFS peak, uniform) [decompiled: V ProcessBlockUp 0x3c49c..0x3c4c8 (0x3804a2b3 = 3.162e-5 at 0x3c4b8)]. Purpose is probably denormal/idle-noise control [guess].
- Mono input: processBlock selects channel indices so a mono bus feeds both lines [decompiled: V processBlock 0x19370..0x193c0] [inferred].

### 1.7 Sample-rate setup
- SetSampleRate returns early if the rate is unchanged, otherwise clears all ten lines, recomputes every coefficient listed above, and does nothing about oversampling [decompiled: V SetSampleRate 0x3bc54..0x3bc84]. Called at every processBlock entry with `this+0x30` (host rate) [decompiled: V processBlock 0x19358..0x19368].

### 1.8 Not determined
- Exact structure of Ocho (gain windows), Tri, VariUp/VariUpDown quantisation, and which tap-multiplier set each mode uses. Tried: scan of constants/offsets per handler, partial instruction reading of Up, Ensemble360, Symphonic, Doubler, TZF+.
- Reported latency (none found). Use of the 100 ms coefficient at base+0x308.

## 2. Flair (Native Instruments)

Binary is stripped of FlangerVoice::process as a standalone symbol; per-sample voice code is inlined in `Flair::processAudio` (0x8d72c, 4176 bytes). Findings are partly [inferred] from instruction shape.

- Voices: `std::array<FlangerVoice<xsimd::batch<double, neon64>, 44100>, 4>`; four voices, double precision, each NEON batch holds the two channels [decompiled: F ni::effektrig::dsp::Flair::setVoiceDelayTimes 0x8c69c (four copies of the delay calc at 0x8c6b8, 0x8c728, 0x8c77c, 0x8c7cc); F std::array<...FlangerVoice...,4ul>::~array 0x8b1c4]. Voice objects at this+0x650, +0x820, +0x9f0, +0xbc0 (stride 0x1d0) [decompiled: F onSetSampleRate 0x8f030..0x8f058].
- Delay time per voice: `delay_seconds = 1 / (f_ref * ratio_i * (1 + spread * c_i))`, `delay_samples = max(delay_seconds * sr_internal - 4, 3)`. `c_i` = {+0.023374, -0.022840, -0.034064, +0.035265} (doubles as immediates at 0x8c6d0, 0x8c730, 0x8c784, 0x8c7d4) [decompiled: F setVoiceDelayTimes 0x8c6e8..0x8c80c]. Pitch confirmation: `ratio_i` come from a chord table at this+0x400 filled by setChordParameter; the delay is the period of a note, so each voice is a comb filter tuned to a pitch [decompiled: F setVoiceDelayTimes; F setChordParameter 0x8b6f8] [inferred]. What `f_ref` (this+0xfa8) and `spread` (this+0xfa0) are is not determined; `spread` is likely a detune/width amount [guess].
- A pitch parameter maps `ratio = 2^((p*96 - 48)/12)`, i.e. ±48 semitones around centre (constants 96.0, -48.0, 1/12 at 0x8c830..0x8c854; same at processAudio 0x8dd38..0x8dd54) [decompiled: F setVoiceDelayTimes 0x8c820..0x8c858]. Which of the float parameters (index 2 of the parameter array) is Pitch was not verified [guess].
- Interpolation: each voice reads four neighbouring delay samples per tap using `f`, `f^2`, `f^3` (explicit `d6*d6`, `d6*d3`) and 0.5 factors, which is the shape of a 4-point cubic (Hermite/Catmull-Rom type) interpolator, evaluated in double [decompiled: F processAudio 0x8e0a0..0x8e250 (4 q loads with indices n-1, n, n+1, n+2 at 0x8e0c0, 0x8e0cc, 0x8e0d0, 0x8e0d8)] [inferred: the exact Hermite coefficients were not matched term by term].
- Two modulated taps per voice: `D + m` and `D - m` (`fadd d4,d2,d3` / `fsub d5,d2,d3`, with `m = lfo * depth`), plus a third read at a second delay value (this+0x668) [decompiled: F processAudio 0x8e098..0x8e1a8]. Taps are mixed with per-voice gains (this+0x690..0x6b0 in voice 0) [decompiled: F processAudio 0x8dfe4..0x8e018].
- Feedback saturation: the feedback signal passes `y = 2 * u / sqrt(1 + u^2)` with `u = 0.5 * x`, built from fmul, fsqrt, fdiv [decompiled: F processAudio 0x8dfb8..0x8dfd0] [inferred reading of the instruction sequence]. Limit ±2.
- LFO: `ni::effektrig::utility::TempoSyncableLFO<double>::process`: a double phase accumulator wrapped into [0,1), free-running or tempo-synced (frequency from note-length and BPM), with an optional shaping callback `std::function` invoked on the phase [decompiled: F TempoSyncableLFO<double>::process 0x8e77c..0x8e8bc]. The waveform shape and the rate range formula are not determined (shape lives behind the std::function; `setLFOMode` exists).
- Scan mode: voice gains from a triangular window of a scan position with sqrt() (equal-power) shaping, three modes by `this+0x550` [decompiled: F setScanModeVoiceGains 0x8bba8..0x8bc7c] [inferred].
- Oversampling and latency: sample-rate ratio is chosen from a table {2.0, 1.0, 0.5, 0.25, 0.125} at 0x1fb70c8 indexed by `int(sr/88200)` clamped to 0..4. So at 44.1 or 48 kHz the engine runs at 2x (up/down converters `NI::AB::SampleRateConverter`, quality 2); at 88.2..176.4 kHz it runs at 1x [decompiled: F onSetSampleRate 0x8edcc..0x8ee60; `python3 $RE/disasm.py "$F" --floats 0x1fb70c8 5`]. `getInternalLatency` returns `2 * [this+0xfd4]` samples when resampling is active, else 0 [decompiled: F getInternalLatency 0x8fc3c]. The AU-reported 0.36 ms (16 samples at 44.1 kHz) therefore matches 2 x 8-sample converter latency [inferred; the value 8 in this+0xfd4 was not read from the code].
- Parameter smoothing: control-rate linear ramps over 16 parameters held as 4-lane float vectors (`state += step * target_delta` with step vector at this+0x3a0), block length about 15 ms and about 10 ms variants (0.015 = 0x3c75c28f at 0x8ef24, 0.01 = 0x3c23d70a at 0x8ef68) [decompiled: F onSetSampleRate 0x8ef24..0x8efa0; processAudio 0x8d97c..0x8da1c] [inferred].
- Width/stereo: two NEON lanes are the two channels; the voice reads use `+m` and `-m` taps, the likely source of decorrelation between channels [inferred]. How the Width parameter weights them was not traced.
- Mode differences (Standard etc.) not determined.
